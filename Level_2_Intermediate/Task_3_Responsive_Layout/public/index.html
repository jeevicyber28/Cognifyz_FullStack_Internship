Standard V60 Method

Four-pour extraction in under three minutes

We dial all filter lots to a 1:16 coffee-to-water ratio (15g medium-fine coffee to 240ml filtered water at 93 °C).

Dose 15g Medium-Fine
Total Water 240ml @ 93 °C
Target Drawdown 2:40 – 2:55
0:00

Bloom (45ml)

Pour 3x coffee weight evenly to saturate grounds, swirl gently, and let CO2 release until 0:40.

0:40

First Pulse (to 130ml)

Pour steadily in concentric circles from center outward without hitting the paper walls.

1:20

Second Pulse (to 190ml)

Maintain a low, steady flow rate to keep slurry temperature consistent.

1:55

Final Pour & Drawdown (to 240ml)

Top up to 240ml, give one gentle Rao spin, and allow full drawdown onto a flat bed.
